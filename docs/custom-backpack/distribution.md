# Distribution

Backpacked Addons are special in the fact they are both a resource and data pack in a single ZIP file, but how do you distribute the file? Both CurseForge and Modrinth have seperate sections for reosurce packs and data packs, **however Backpacked addons should be simply treated as a resource pack**. This means in both the [CurseForge App (standalone)](https://www.curseforge.com/download/app) and [Modrinth App](https://modrinth.com/app), Backpacked Addons can be installed with one-click into players' game profiles.

## CurseForge

### Creating a Resource Pack project

1. In the [Authors Dashboard](https://authors.curseforge.com/), navigate to **Projects** in the left sidebar, then click `Start a Project` button in the top right corner, or click this [handy link](https://authors.curseforge.com/#/projects/create/choose-game) to quickly start creating a new project.

2. Select `Minecraft` as the game your project is designed for. *Ensure that it is `Minecraft`, and not `Minecraft Bedrock`.*

3. Fill in the required **Project Name**, **Logo**, and **Summary** fields. The summary should mention that it is an addon for Backpacked. Using the keyword **Backpacked** in your summary will allow users to use the search functionality of CurseForge to filter resource packs for Backpacked addons. *E.g. if the user searched for "backpacked", your addon will appear in the search results since the keyword appears in your summary*.

![Test](/img/curseforge_summary.png)

4. For the **Class** field, select `Resource Packs`. 

![Test](/img/curseforge_class.png)

5. For the **Main Category** field, select the resoultion you used for the backpack textures. If the resoultion matches Minecraft's texture, you likely used **16x**.

6. For the **Additional Categories** field, select `Mod Support` and `Data Packs` tags as these best match a Backpacked addon. 

![Test](/img/curseforge_categories.png)

7. Click **Next** at the bottom right, then fill in the **Description**. You should clearly mention that this is a Backpacked Addon to not confuse users who may come across your project.

8. Click **Next** at the bottom right, and then you'll be prompted to Select a **Project License**. It is your choice on how you'll license your addon, however a more open license will allow other addon developers to learn from your work.

9. For **Project Distribution**, allowing your project to be available to 3rd parties will allow for a wider reach but may slightly affect reward point generation. This is your decision on what you would like to do.

10. Finally click **Create** at the bottom right.
